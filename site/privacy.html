Privacy and cookies

School Compare is a demo with synthetic school data. No real school receives your requests.

What this site stores in your browser

  • sca_variant: which version of the site you see (A or B), so it stays the same on every visit.
  • sca_consent: your cookie choice, so the banner does not ask again.

Analytics

Analytics and advertising storage start as denied. Analytics cookies are used only after you choose "Accept all". You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What is never sent

Nothing you type in the "Request info" form (email, grade, start year or message) is sent to analytics. Only whether the form was submitted and whether its optional fields were filled.
